A Day at School No. 9

You woke up on a stage. Something rang in your head all night. The Warden hears everything — except, apparently, you.

An original dialogue-and-skill-check RPG. Headphones recommended.

Before you wake up fully

  • Move & look. Click (or tap) the floor to walk. Hover people and objects to see them glow green; touch users tap once to preview, twice to act. Tab or the ◉ button shows everything.
  • Listen to your head. Your 24 skills talk to you. Strong skills interrupt with passive observations; weak ones stay quiet.
  • Roll the dice. Skill checks are 2d6 + skill. White checks can be retried after the skill rises; red checks only once.
  • Spend points. XP becomes skill points in Character (C). The Thoughts tab (T) costs time and changes you.
  • Mind the clock. Exam at 17:00. Curfew at 21:00. Defiance raises Watched; at 100 you are arrested. There are six endings.
