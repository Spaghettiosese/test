You woke up on a stage. Something rang in your head all night. The Warden hears everything — except, apparently, you.

An original dialogue-and-skill-check RPG. Headphones recommended.
Orientation leaflet · No. 9

Before you wake up fully

  1. Move & look. Click (or tap) the floor to walk. Hover people and objects to see them glow green; touch users tap once to preview, twice to act. Tab or the eye button shows everything.
  2. Listen to your head. Your 24 skills talk to you. Strong skills interrupt with passive observations; weak ones stay quiet.
  3. Roll the dice. Skill checks are 2d6 + skill. White checks can be retried after the skill rises; red checks only once.
  4. Spend points. XP becomes skill points in Character (C). The Thoughts tab (T) costs time and changes you.
  5. Mind the clock. Exam at 17:00. Curfew at 21:00. Defiance raises Watched; at 100 you are arrested. There are six endings.
