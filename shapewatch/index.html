HOW TO PLAY

Two teams of five fight over a payload. Attackers push it down Frostgate Street to the goal before the clock runs out; defenders stand on it to stop it. Reach a checkpoint and the clock refills.

THE TWISTS

Rift Surges. Every minute or so the storm rewrites the rules for half a minute: low gravity, overclocked cooldowns, glass-cannon damage or a payload surge.

Echo Cores. Every elimination drops a core. Grab one for your team to gain ultimate charge and a burst of health.

Skins. Pick a palette on the hero select screen; the 3D model updates live.
