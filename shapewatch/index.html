HOW TO PLAY

Two teams of five (or eight heroes in a free-for-all) fight over an objective. Pick a hero, learn their kit, and use the systems below: roles, subclasses, crits, pings and the comm wheel, echo cores and rift surges.

SYSTEMS

Roles and subclasses. Tanks, Damage and Support, each split into subclasses (Bruiser, Initiator, Stalwart · Flanker, Sharpshooter, Specialist, Recon · Medic, Tactician, Survivor) with their own strengths and counters.

Crits. Headshots always crit. Every hero also has a chance to crit on body shots (gold numbers and a bright hit marker).

Pings and comms. Ping enemies, health packs and objectives. Your bot team reacts to your pings and callouts, and calls out the same things back, in their own voices.

Kill cam and Play of the Game. See how you died through the killer's eyes. After the match, the best moment is replayed.

Echo cores, rift surges, medals and challenges keep every match different. Your career is saved on this device.
