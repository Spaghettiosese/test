# Hollowmere: what is in the game

This is the running list for the second update ("The Wide Valley"). Every line is something you can find or do in the build. The first update's systems (town, keep, schedules, stealth, skills, cutscenes) are listed at the end.

## The world (about five times the ground of the first demo)

1. The Old Road runs 260 m south of Ashgate, with a milestone, signposts and a waystone.
2. A wrecked wagon with a lootable crate sits on the road.
3. Tolliver Farms: a furnished farmhouse with a hearth, bed and locked chest.
4. A barn with hay bales, three bunks, a feed chest and a fire.
5. A locked tool shed with a chest of picks and salves.
6. Wheat, turnip and furrow fields that you can walk through.
7. Fenced pastures with gaps for the paths.
8. A scarecrow.
9. A farm well.
10. The Greywater river cuts across the whole map.
11. A stone bridge with lamps over the river.
12. A toll house with a locked door, bunks, table and strongbox.
13. A toll beam and banner across the bridge.
14. The Mirewood: hundreds of solid pines, birches and dead trees.
15. Undergrowth: ferns, logs, stumps and boulders.
16. Hunter's lodge with a chest and a hearth.
17. A ruined watch tower with a crenellated top and a locked cache.
18. The witch's hut, raised on stilts over the bog.
19. A bandit camp with tents, bedrolls, log seats, a fire, a palisade and loot chests.
20. Blackfen swamp: seven deep pools you cannot cross.
21. A boardwalk network that snakes over the fen.
22. The sunken saint's shrine with a drowned reliquary.
23. Will-o-wisp lights drifting over the bog.
24. Reed clumps and dead sticks in the fen.
25. Cinderwick: eight burnt-out houses with broken walls and charred beams.
26. The plague ward: a fenced graveyard with 24 graves and a mass grave to dig through.
27. Hangman's Hill with a gallows, platform and locked kit.
28. Four waystones that fast-travel between each other.
29. Nine dirt trails link every landmark.
30. A dense treeline closes the map edge.
31. Colliders stream in and out around you, so a 1,500-piece forest costs almost nothing.
32. Six named regions announce themselves as you enter.
33. Each region has its own ambience: frogs in the fen, owls in the wood, roosters on the farm, whispers in Cinderwick, rushing water at the bridge.
34. Each region has its own score: separate chord sets for the fen, the wood and the ash.
35. Fireflies rise in the woods and fen at night.
36. Wading through the bog splashes.
37. A fog-of-war world map paints itself as you explore (M).
38. A round minimap sits under the clock.
39. The map labels landmarks once you have seen them.
40. The map and compass mark your current goal and the waystones.
41. Fires outdoors crackle when you are near them.
42. Distant hills are pushed out to frame the bigger valley.
43. Camera far plane is tuned to the fog so the bigger world stays cheap.

## People (80 of them, up from 59)

44. Farmer Tolliver walks his fields by day and sleeps in the barn.
45. Maren Tolliver cooks, draws water and eats at the table.
46. Two farmhands work different fields.
47. Toll Captain Bosk keeps a desk and a strongbox key.
48. Three toll guards: a sentry, a crossbow lookout and a patrol.
49. Gil the peddler walks the road by day and sells wares.
50. A pilgrim walks between waystones.
51. Hermit Oswin warms himself by the Mirewood waystone.
52. Hunter Wulf patrols the woods and sleeps in his lodge.
53. Old Sable the witch works, warms and sleeps on a schedule.
54. Red Cael, a tougher bandit chief with a blocking sword.
55. Six more bandits: diners, sleepers, a lookout, a patrol and a gate guard.
56. Ilse, the last soul in Cinderwick.
57. Highwaymen ambush you on the roads at night.

## Enemy behaviour

58. A squad director hands out tactics to every guard that is hunting you.
59. Only two swords (three at high alarm) engage you at once.
60. The rest circle you and strafe.
61. Flankers move to your side.
62. Cut-off guards run ahead of your escape route.
63. Guards chase where you were heading, not just where you were.
64. Crossbow guards hold range and shoot bolts with lead.
65. Bolts can be deflected by a parry and stick in walls.
66. Searching guards split up and pick different rooms and hiding spots.
67. Searchers check chests and open shut doors.
68. Search time grows with the alarm level.
69. Guards feint before a real strike.
70. Guards chain a second swing.
71. Some strikes are shield shoves that break your block.
72. Guards side-step your swings.
73. Wounded guards retreat toward allies.
74. Half-dead guards shout for reinforcements and raise the alarm.
75. Guards find knocked-out bodies and wake them.
76. Guards chat to each other on watch.
77. Guards fidget: look around, fold arms.
78. Guards and villagers turn their heads to look at you.
79. Villagers who see a crime run to a guard to report it.
80. Kill the witness before they arrive and there is no bounty.
81. Guards recognise a wanted face from a distance.
82. Disguised players are challenged if they linger.
83. Brute hollows: huge, slow and hard-hitting.
84. Screamer hollows wake every sleeper nearby.
85. Midnight raises hollows around the graveyard and Cinderwick.
86. After the theft, hollows keep clawing out of the ground near you.
87. Rain and fog shorten how far guards see.
88. Three difficulty levels change damage and vision.

## Combat and tools

89. Riposte: a parry sets up a double-damage counter.
90. Kick (Q) shoves and staggers, even through a guard.
91. Heavy blows cause bleeding.
92. Hit-stop on every blow.
93. Slow motion on parries and assassinations.
94. Camera shake on heavy hits.
95. Throwing knives (G) with headshots.
96. An unaware headshot is a silent kill.
97. Thrown knives stick in walls and can be picked up again.
98. Coin toss (V) lures guards to the noise.
99. Fire flasks (X) start a spreading blaze.
100. Burning people scream, panic and take damage.
101. Poisoned blade (5) for five coated hits.
102. Poisoned targets cough and die slowly.
103. The lead sap (B) knocks out the unwary instead of killing.
104. Knocked-out people can be searched.
105. Drag bodies (hold Z) to hide them.
106. Pickpocketing (hold E from behind) with a risk meter.
107. Getting caught pickpocketing raises the alarm.
108. Wraith Sight (4) shows everyone through walls.
109. Sight shows guard cones with the right perk.
110. Ember flasks and salves now have crafting recipes.

## Progression and crafting

111. Experience and levels.
112. Kills, quests, discoveries, lore, crafting and pickpocketing all pay XP.
113. Silent kills pay bonus XP.
114. Fifteen perks across Shadow, Blade, Body, Ember and Alchemy trees.
115. Shadowstep: quieter footsteps.
116. Cutthroat: silent backstabs.
117. Lockmaster: faster picks.
118. Nimble Fingers: better pickpocketing.
119. Sharpened Edge: sword damage.
120. Iron Grip: a longer parry window.
121. Second Wind: heal on kill.
122. Knifework: stronger, recoverable knives.
123. Thick Skin: more health.
124. Fleet of Foot: stamina.
125. Ember Well: more Ember.
126. Umbral Focus: faster skill recharge.
127. Toxicology: stronger poison.
128. Firebug: bigger fires.
129. Wraith Eyes: longer, sharper sight.
130. Seven crafting recipes (poison, fire flasks, knives, salve, Ember flask, picks, sap).
131. Selling loot to smiths, peddlers and witches.
132. New shop stock: knives, poison, fire flasks, sap.
133. Books can be read for XP.
134. Fires heal and refill Ember.

## Law, disguise and consequences

135. Crimes are only crimes if someone sees them.
136. Bounties per faction (Watch, Keep, Bandits).
137. Bounties fade when you stay out of sight.
138. Guards offer to take a fine and forget your face.
139. Wanted status shows on the HUD.
140. Theft is witnessed when you open a chest in view.
141. Lockpicking in view is a crime.
142. Assault and murder of villagers are witnessed.
143. Arson is a crime.
144. Strip a uniform off a dead guard to disguise yourself.
145. Watch and Keep uniforms fool different people.
146. Running, fighting or restricted rooms blow your cover.
147. Disguises can be dropped with U.
